# Electricity Department SOP

## Streetlight outage
SLA: 48 hours. Escalation: electrical supervisor after 24 hours.
Ownership: municipal streetlights route to Electricity; feeder faults route to the distribution utility.
Closure verification: light operates for a full night cycle and no exposed wiring remains.

## Live or fallen wire
SLA: 1 hour. Escalation: emergency utility line, fire service, and police immediately.
Safety critical: always critical. Maintain a 10-meter exclusion zone until a licensed crew clears it.
Closure verification: utility authorization confirms de-energization and physical repair.

## Transformer smoke or fire
SLA: 1 hour. Escalation: fire service, utility control room, and incident commander immediately.
Ownership: Electricity coordinates municipal equipment; distribution assets route to the utility.
Closure verification: fire is out, utility reconnects safely, and an electrical inspection passes.

## Power outage cluster
SLA: 6 hours. Escalation: district utility operations after 3 hours.
Closure verification: supply is restored to the cluster and two utility readings confirm stability.

## Exposed utility junction box
SLA: 2 hours. Escalation: utility duty officer immediately and Roads for a traffic barrier.
Safety critical: critical if accessible to children or pedestrians.
Closure verification: cover is secured, grounding is tested, and the utility inspector closes the ticket.
