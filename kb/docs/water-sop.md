# Water Department SOP

## Water leak repair
SLA: 24 hours. Escalation: supervisor after 12 hours; public works officer after 24 hours.
Ownership: Water Department owns pipes and valves in public roads. Re-route private-side leaks to Citizen's utility provider.
Closure verification: repaired pipe or valve must have two pressure readings and one citizen confirmation.

## Burst main and flooding
SLA: 4 hours. Escalation: duty engineer immediately; emergency operations for flooding or traffic hazard.
Safety critical: report as critical if flooding reaches homes, an underpass, or a school.
Closure verification: flow stops, public area is safe, and service pressure is restored.

## Contaminated supply
SLA: 2 hours. Escalation: public health immediately and incident commander for boil-water notice.
Ownership: Water investigates source; Public Health owns public advisories.
Closure verification: bacteriological test passes and Public Health clears the advisory.

## Low pressure cluster
SLA: 48 hours. Escalation: district engineer after 24 hours for a ward-wide cluster.
Ownership: meter-only disputes require Water billing triage before field dispatch.
Closure verification: pressure meets local service threshold at the reported and adjacent addresses.

## Meter and billing leak dispute
SLA: 72 hours. Escalation: billing supervisor after 48 hours.
Ownership: physical pipe defects remain with Water; meter accuracy requires the utility billing cell.
Closure verification: leak is ruled out or meter testing is documented and the rerouted owner closes the work order.
