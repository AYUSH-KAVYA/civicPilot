# Public Health Department SOP

## Vector breeding complaint
SLA: 24 hours. Escalation: vector-control officer after 12 hours and district medical officer for clusters.
Ownership: Public Health assesses disease risk. Parks or Sanitation removes source where applicable.
Closure verification: inspection shows no active breeding sites and treatment evidence is recorded.

## Food safety hazard
SLA: 4 hours. Escalation: inspector and district health officer immediately.
Safety critical: critical for suspected widespread illness or vulnerable-community exposure.
Closure verification: premises comply or are closed; affected people receive public guidance.

## Clinic access issue
SLA: 24 hours. Escalation: facility administrator after 12 hours.
Ownership: Public Health owns public clinics. Re-route municipal infrastructure failures to Roads, Water, or Electricity.
Closure verification: access and essential service are restored and patient safety is confirmed.

## Communicable disease cluster
SLA: 2 hours. Escalation: district surveillance team and emergency operations immediately.
Ownership: Public Health leads. Do not disclose protected case details in public notifications.
Closure verification: epidemiology team closes the incident after containment and reporting requirements are met.

## Heat or cold exposure cluster
SLA: 2 hours. Escalation: medical director and emergency operations immediately.
Safety critical: critical for vulnerable residents or a public shelter with disrupted utilities.
Closure verification: shelter capacity and essential services are restored and risk advisories are withdrawn.
