# Sanitation Department SOP

## Missed garbage collection
SLA: 24 hours. Escalation: zone supervisor after 12 hours.
Ownership: Sanitation owns municipal collection points. Re-route private estates to the estate manager.
Closure verification: waste is removed and bin area is clean.

## Sewage overflow
SLA: 4 hours. Escalation: public health and sewer duty engineer immediately.
Safety critical: critical near homes, hospitals, schools, or food establishments.
Closure verification: flow stops, contamination is cleaned, and Public Health clears reoccupation.

## Illegal dumping cluster
SLA: 48 hours. Escalation: enforcement team after 24 hours.
Ownership: dump on public land belongs to Sanitation; private land routes to enforcement and property owner.
Closure verification: waste is removed and disposal receipt and photo evidence are attached.

## Public toilet maintenance
SLA: 24 hours. Escalation: facilities supervisor after 12 hours.
Closure verification: sanitation, water, lighting, and accessibility checks are documented.

## Sharps or hazardous waste
SLA: 2 hours. Escalation: hazardous-waste team and public health immediately.
Safety critical: critical around schools, hospitals, markets, or occupied public areas.
Closure verification: licensed disposal receipt and a final hazard-area inspection are attached.
