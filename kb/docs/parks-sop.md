# Parks Department SOP

## Fallen tree on public land
SLA: 4 hours. Escalation: emergency operations and Roads if traffic is blocked.
Safety critical: critical near roads, hospitals, schools, power lines, or occupied buildings.
Closure verification: hazard is removed and the area is inspected for secondary instability.

## Park equipment damage
SLA: 72 hours. Escalation: recreation officer after 48 hours.
Ownership: Parks owns municipal playgrounds. Re-route equipment on school grounds to the school authority.
Closure verification: equipment passes inspection or is isolated with a visible safety notice.

## Irrigation leak
SLA: 48 hours. Escalation: horticulture supervisor after 24 hours.
Re-routing: mainline water waste goes to Water; irrigation-only leaks stay with Parks.
Closure verification: leak stops and the turf/planting remains safe.

## Mosquito breeding in park
SLA: 24 hours. Escalation: public health vector-control team immediately for a cluster.
Ownership: Parks removes standing water; Public Health decides disease-control measures.
Closure verification: breeding sites are eliminated and Public Health is notified.

## Unsafe mature tree
SLA: 6 hours. Escalation: arborist and emergency operations immediately.
Safety critical: critical if the tree is leaning over occupied property, a school, or a power line.
Closure verification: arborist inspection documents stabilization or removal and the area is reopened.
