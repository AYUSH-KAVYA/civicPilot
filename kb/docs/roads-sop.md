# Roads Department SOP

## Pothole repair
SLA: 72 hours. Escalation: ward roads officer after 48 hours.
Ownership: Roads owns public streets and footpaths; private or toll roads route to the concession owner.
Closure verification: pothole is filled, area is compacted, and a photo check passes.

## Severe pothole or road collapse
SLA: 4 hours. Escalation: traffic police and emergency operations immediately.
Safety critical: classify critical if a deep collapse, exposed reinforcement, or injury risk affects traffic.
Closure verification: barricades and temporary repair remain effective before reopening.

## Fallen road sign
SLA: 24 hours. Escalation: road safety cell after 12 hours.
Ownership: Roads owns municipal signs; national signage routes to the traffic authority.
Closure verification: sign is restored or safely removed and fasteners are secure.

## Footpath obstruction
SLA: 48 hours. Escalation: ward officer after 36 hours.
Ownership: accessibility obstructions near schools or hospitals receive priority re-routing.
Closure verification: obstruction is removed and an accessible path remains open.

## Bridge guardrail damage
SLA: 8 hours. Escalation: structures engineer immediately and traffic police for lane closure.
Safety critical: critical if a guardrail is missing near traffic or a pedestrian route.
Closure verification: structural inspection passes and lane or pedestrian controls are removed safely.
